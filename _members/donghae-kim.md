Donghae Kim is an Assistant Professor in Robotics & Mechatronics Engineering at DGIST. He received the Ph.D. in aerospace engineering from the University of Texas at Austin, Austin, TX, in 2026, where he also completed the postdoctoral training at the department of Aerospace Engineering and Engineering Mechanics. He received the B.Sc. and M.S. degrees in aerospace engineering from Seoul National University, Seoul, Republic of Korea, in 2014 and 2016, respectively. He worked as a Research Engineer with Hyundai Motor Company from 2016 to 2019, and a Robotics Engineer with Seoul Robotics from 2019 to 2021.
