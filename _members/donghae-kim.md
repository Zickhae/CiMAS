Donghae Kim is an Assistant Professor in Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering at DGIST. He received his Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering from the University of Texas at Austin in 2026 and subsequently conducted postdoctoral research in its Department of Aerospace Engineering and Engineering Mechanics. He earned his B.Sc. and M.S. degrees in Aerospace Engineering from Seoul National University in 2014 and 2016, respectively. Before pursuing his Ph.D., he worked as a Research Engineer at Hyundai Motor Company from 2016 to 2019 and as a Robotics Engineer at Seoul Robotics from 2019 to 2021.
