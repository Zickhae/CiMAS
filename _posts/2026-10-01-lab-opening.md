Welcome to the Control Group for Intelligent Multi-agent Systems (CiMAS)! We study scalable, robust guidance, navigation, and control frameworks, alongside individual agent interactions, to achieve resilient autonomy in multi-agent systems. The lab opens in the [Department of Robotics and Mechatronics Engineering](https://www.dgist.ac.kr/robot/) at DGIST in October 2026.
